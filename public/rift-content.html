Seb Prime 100

The Seb Prime 100 is an astronomical phenomenon that lies under the thick layers of Saturn's rings. It has been officially classified as the 147th moon of the planet and was the object of exploration missions after the "Time Anomaly" discovery.


Its position at the unique Lagrangian point in the orbit of Saturn makes it almost impossible to be seen with the naked eye. The dense core, although smaller than Mars, possesses distinct physical qualities that have no precedent in the entire universe.

This is the most valuable resource of our planet. Since there is very strong gravity, one Earth day is equivalent to two weeks on Seb Prime 100 – the perfect place for vacations.

The Portal and The Observer's Pact

Technology

This portal is referred to as The Rift; it is a tear in spacetime. This mode of transportation does not require fuel but relies on the geothermal energy of the planet.

Transit Time

The transit period lasts precisely 3.7 seconds. It is a time of "suspension": one experiences merely a mild sensation of cold and hears a low-frequency sound.

Security

All creatures passing through are scanned by the system. In case any bad intent or toxins are found, passage is denied. The planet defends itself.

The Observer's Pact

Prior to entering the planet, everyone must sign the Pact, an environmentally friendly agreement. Not even a trace of one’s presence can be left.

Energy Cycle

This volcano side of the planet holds an enormous amount of lava that flows underneath the ocean side. This acts as an environmental heat source, keeping the temperature of the oceans steady at 28°C.

Resource Cycle

It is from the convergence of fire and water that the most pristine volcanic glass (obsidian) can be found. Automated drones harvest it for use in construction of the Earth colonies.

Atmospheric Cycle

These gigantic bioluminescent coral reefs on the ocean side of Earth serve as a planetary filter by absorbing harmful volcanic gases and producing oxygen.

The Architects - Mystery of Disappearance

Identity

The Architects sound like this ancient civilization that figured out how to engineer whole planets in ways that feel almost impossible today. Modern science calls it magic level stuff. I think they were not like biological beings at all, since there are no skeletons or any genetic traces left behind.

Constructions

That makes me wonder about what they built. Beyond just the portal, there is this whole underground system of corridors they called Veins. They pulse with a soft light, and it reacts to how travelers are feeling emotionally. The planet itself seems alive because of them, like their presence is still there somehow.

Transcedence

All the signs point to them not dying out in the usual way. Instead, they reached transcendence or something. They left behind physical bodies and moved their consciousness right into the planets structure. That is how they turned into Seb Prime 100.

The Inscription

In the main terminal for the portal, there are these words engraved on some metal no one recognizes. The language is still undeciphered. Dr. Mia came up with a translation though. It says:

"We will return when the lesson has been learned."

That part gets a bit confusing, like what lesson exactly.
