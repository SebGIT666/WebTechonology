PRIVACY POLICY

Cosmic Divide - Data Protection Statement

What Data we Collect

Cosmic Divide collects only the minimum data necessary to process your enquiry through our contact form:


: Full name

: Email address

: Subject of enquiry

: Message content


We do not collect or store any extra personal data on this platform. It is not shared either. No one is using tracking stuff or third party analytics here. Cosmic Divide keeps it simple like that.

Cookies

About cookies, they are not for tracking or ads at all. The only kind we have are those essential session ones. You know, the ones needed just to make the basic features work. And those get deleted right away when you shut down your browser. That covers the main points, but it might be worth checking if anything changes.

How We Protect Your Data

All data submitted through the contact form is stored securely and used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell, trade or transfer your personal information to any third parties.


Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to process your request and is deleted thereafter.

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:


: Access the personal data we hold about you

: Request correction of inaccurate data

: Request deletion of your data

: Whitdraw consent at any time


To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the Contact page.
