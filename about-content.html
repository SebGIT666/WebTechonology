History of Cosmic Divide

Seb Prime 100 is this weird moon around Saturn, kind of hidden in the rings, and its officially the 147th one there. I think what makes it special is this time anomaly they found, which turned it into a spot for vacations that feel really long. Like, one day back on Earth is two weeks there because of the gravity messing with time, its a 1 to 14 ratio or something. The place is smaller than Mars but has this dense core that explains why its so different.


They spotted it back in 2024 with some probe called Aeon, picking up radio signals that had math patterns in them, strange stuff. A manned team went about ten years later and the planet was just empty, but everything worked like it was set up for people to show up. Its at this odd Lagrange point in Saturns orbit, so you cant see it easily without gear.


The portal there is not like any engine we know, its more of a rift in space time that pulls energy from the planets heat. Taking the trip lasts 3.7 seconds exactly, and you feel a chill and hear this low hum, sort of suspended. It scans you first, and if theres bad intentions or toxins, it wont let you through, which seems safe enough.

Our Mission - Eco Friendly Exploration

Seb Prime 100 is not an ordinary colony, it is the most precious ecosystem in the known cosmos. Our mission is Zero Footprint.


The Observer's Pack

Before you go in, theres this Observers Pact you have to agree to, its about not messing up the place, no litter or changing the land because its a closed system and everything is balanced. That part gets a bit strict, I guess to keep the symbiosis going.

Harmony With Nature

The planet has two sides split by the Rift, one all fire and volcanoes, the other water with oceans. The lava from the fire side flows under the water and keeps it warm at 28 degrees Celsius, which is pretty constant. Where they meet, you get this obsidian glass formed, and drones collect it for building stuff on Earth colonies.


In the water hemisphere, theres these huge glowing coral reefs that filter out bad gases from the volcanoes and turn them into oxygen, its like a big cycle for the air. The whole energy and resource thing ties together, fire meeting water and all that.

The Architects

Who built it though, the Architects, thats the mystery. No bones or DNA around, but they engineered the planet like magic, way beyond what we can do. Besides the portal, theres these underground Veins, tunnels that light up soft based on how you feel, reacting to emotions.
